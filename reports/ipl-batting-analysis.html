IPL Batting Statistics Analysis

Explored player batting records and used six visualizations to examine how runs, strike rate and boundary hitting varied across players.

Data and method

The batting dataset included player, matches, runs, average, strike rate, centuries, half-centuries, fours, sixes and highest score. I used pandas to inspect, clean and summarize the CSV, then explored sorted results, groupings and correlations.

I used Matplotlib and Seaborn for visualization and built an introductory linear regression model with scikit-learn to demonstrate how supervised learning could be applied to the dataset.

Visualizations

  • Total runs by player
  • Runs scored by the top 10 players
  • Sixes hit by player
  • Strike rate compared with runs
  • Distribution of runs across players
  • Run analysis heatmap

Outcome

The charts let me examine both individual totals and broader patterns in the batting data. The regression model was an introductory demonstration. The original project slides did not record a final error score.
